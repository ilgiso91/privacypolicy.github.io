Privacy Policy - Safa

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Safa ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Safa mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App"). Safa is operated by Ismail Turanli. You can contact us at ismailturanli91@gmail.com.

Safa is an Islamic companion app that may provide prayer times, Qibla direction, Quran reading and recitation, dhikr tools, Ramadan guidance, Jumuah preparation, Yasin Sharif, Asma ul Husna, prayer guidance, nearby mosque information, notifications, and an AI-assisted question feature ("Ask Imam").

Safa does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.

By downloading, installing, accessing, or using Safa, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Support communications: If you contact us, we may receive your email address, message content, and any information you choose to include.
  • App preferences: You may choose settings such as calculation preferences, notification preferences, selected city or mosque, Quran reading progress, saved tools, or similar app choices. These are stored on your device.
  • Ask Imam questions: If you use Ask Imam, the question you type is sent to our AI service provider to generate an answer. See Section 3.
  • Reported AI answers: If you use the "Report" option on an AI answer, that answer and the question it replied to are sent to us for review. See Section 3.
  • Promotional codes: In the Android app, you can enter a promotional code we provide to receive premium access for a limited time. Our server checks the code without storing it. To limit guessing, it records the number of attempts linked to your anonymous ID and to a one-way keyed hash of your network address, and deletes these records within 2 days. If the code is valid, premium access is granted through RevenueCat, and our server records when it was granted and when it ends, and deletes that record after 30 days.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, app version, language, region, and similar technical information.
  • Usage information: App interactions such as screens viewed, sessions, and a small number of in-app events (for example, when the premium screen is shown or closed, when prayer times are first viewed, and whether a permission was granted). These events do not include your name, contact details, or the text you read or type.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs, error reports, and related device state used to find and fix problems.
  • App-specific identifiers: Random identifiers created by our service providers: the Google Analytics for Firebase app instance ID, the Firebase Crashlytics installation ID, a RevenueCat customer ID, and, in the Android app, an anonymous Firebase Authentication ID created the first time you use Ask Imam. These identifiers are not linked to your name or contact details.
  • Network information: Like any internet service, our servers and service providers receive your IP address when the App connects to them. Google Analytics derives an approximate location (such as country or city) from a masked IP address. Our AI server keeps only a one-way keyed hash of your network address, for rate limiting, and deletes it within 2 days.
  • Purchase information: If you buy a subscription or other premium access, we receive the product, price, purchase date, and subscription status from Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat. Google Analytics also records in-app purchase events. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

No advertising identifiers. Safa does not collect the Android advertising ID or the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA), does not show ads, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1.3 Location Information

Safa may request access to your location to calculate prayer times, show Qibla direction, find nearby mosques, and schedule location-based reminders. Depending on your device permissions and app settings, location may come from your device's GPS, from approximate location, or from a city you select manually.

Your coordinates are used on your device for these calculations. To show the name of your city, the App asks your device's built-in geocoding service (provided by Apple on iOS and by Google on Android) to convert the coordinates into a place name. On iOS, nearby mosque results come from Apple Maps; on Android, the App opens your maps app at your selected location when you ask for nearby mosques. Safa does not send your coordinates to its own servers and does not keep a history of your location.

You can control location access through your device settings. If you deny or disable location access, you can select your city manually; some features may work less precisely.

1.4 Notifications

If you enable notifications, the App may use your device notification permission to send prayer reminders, Jumuah reminders, Ramadan-related reminders, or other app notifications. These reminders are scheduled on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

1.5 Quran Audio, Translations, and Pronunciations

When you play or download Quran recitations, download a translation, or play an Asma ul Husna pronunciation, the App may connect to the content sources EveryAyah (everyayah.com), Al Quran Cloud (alquran.cloud), and Wikimedia Commons. As with any web request, these sources receive your IP address and the file being requested. We do not send them any other information about you.

3. AI-Assisted Features (Ask Imam)

Ask Imam answers questions about Islam using an AI model provided by OpenAI. Using it is optional.

  • What is sent: The question you type and your app language. In the iOS app, up to the last 12 messages of the same conversation are also sent so that the answer can follow the conversation; in the Android app, only your latest question is sent. Your name, contact details, and location are not sent.
  • How it is sent: In the Android app, questions go to our server on Google Firebase (Cloud Functions, European Union, Frankfurt region), which forwards them to OpenAI. In the iOS app, questions are sent to OpenAI through AIProxy, a service that protects our OpenAI access key.
  • What OpenAI keeps: OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models by default. It may keep requests and answers in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, unless the law requires longer. For Android requests, we ask OpenAI not to store the conversation, and we send a one-way hashed identifier instead of any ID that identifies you, so that OpenAI can detect abuse.
  • Content checks: In the Android app, our server also sends your question and the generated answer to OpenAI's moderation service, which flags harmful content. If either is flagged, you receive a short notice instead of an answer. Only the type of content that was flagged is logged, never the text.
  • What our server keeps: Our server does not store the text of your questions or of the answers. It keeps only technical usage records linked to an anonymous ID (such as how many questions were asked today, whether premium access is active, a request ID, and the size of the request), and deletes them automatically within 30 days.
  • Reported answers: If you report an answer (available in the Android app), we store that answer, the question it replied to, your app language, and the time of the report, together with a one-way hashed reporter ID. We use reports only to review answers and improve the feature, and delete them automatically after 90 days. You can report up to 10 answers a day.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information, confidential information, financial information, medical information, legal information, or information about others into Ask Imam. AI-generated answers may be inaccurate or incomplete and should not be treated as religious rulings (fatwa), legal advice, medical advice, financial advice, or professional counseling.

In the iOS app, requests pass through AIProxy (see the AIProxy Privacy Policy).

4.6 Content Sources
